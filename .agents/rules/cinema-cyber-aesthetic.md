# CINEMA STUDENT PORTFOLIO — AESTHETIC & ARCHITECTURAL RULES

> Full specification and design system in [GEMINI.md](../../GEMINI.md).

## Key Directives:
1. **OLED Black Only (`#000000`)**: Never use light gray or dark blue background fills.
2. **Liquid Chrome & Molten Metal**: Specular highlights, metallic gradients, 4-point star glints (`✧`), chrome typography.
3. **Tactile Optical Refraction**: Fluted/ribbed glass, lenticular blurs, chromatic aberration.
4. **Thermal / Heatmap Gradients**: Infrared spectrums (cobalt blue `#0022FF` to ember orange `#FF5500` and cyan `#00F0FF`).
5. **Authentic Film HUDs**: Strict anamorphic 2.39:1 / 4:3 viewfinders, timecodes (`00:00:00:00`), `REC [●]`, camera specs (`ARRI ALEXA MINI LF`, `ISO 800`, `24FPS`).
6. **Motion & Transitions**: Exclusively use `transitions-dev` tokens (tilt `19-card-tilt`, modal `06-modal`, text swap `04-text-states-swap`, skeleton `14-skeleton-reveal`).
7. **Agentation & ECC Harmony**: Retain semantic accessibility tags and structured TDD workflows.
